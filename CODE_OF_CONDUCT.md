# Code of Conduct

We want VOKO's community to be constructive, welcoming, and safe for contributors and users.

Be respectful, discuss technical disagreements in good faith, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or publication of another person's private information. Do not submit credentials, personal data, or content you do not have the right to share.

Report conduct concerns privately to `dev@vokovoko.com`. Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates this code of conduct.
